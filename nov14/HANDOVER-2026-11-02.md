## 2. What the page is

`nov14/index.html` is a capture of the live Blueprint course page (markup plus the computed stylesheet `exact-source-design.css`), with the November promo layered on top. It loads three stylesheets from the production asset host that are not in the repo (`r15-sheet.css`, `r15-hero.css`, `r15-folds.css`) plus the fonts from Google Fonts (Playfair Display, Inter, Instrument Serif) and Codec Pro from cdnfonts.

Runtime scripts that rewrite copy on load, in this order:

1. `nov14-copy.js`: pillar card descriptions, syllabus headline ("The 18-class syllabus"), CGM headline ("Abbott Lingo sensor and app"), hero CTA, promo strip text and the strip's Enroll button.
2. `nov14-flow.js`: hero headline ("Build your personal longevity protocol in 18 live classes."), turns every "Enroll now" link into an enrol-modal trigger, mock bar.
3. `nov14-pricing.js`, `nov14-promo.js`: price values and promo copy for the pricing panel and modal.

Do not add a fourth copy script. Change copy in the HTML or in these files.

All page-specific CSS added for the promo lives in two inline style blocks in `index.html`: `<style id="nov14-fold">` (pricing panel, mobile rules) and `<style id="nov14-v2">` (upright type, Julie fold, CGM band, syllabus colours). Both sit at the end of `<head>`.

## 5. Type and colour rules

- Fonts are the production fonts: Playfair Display for headings and body, Inter for UI, plus the masterclass pair (Codec Pro, Instrument Serif) inside the Julie fold only. Do not change font families, sizes outside the folds above, colours or the logo.
- Everything is upright. The captured stylesheet had `font-style: italic` on `body` and all headings, plus a dozen forced-italic rules; all were set to normal. The one exception is `em` accents inside headlines (blue, italic), which are the production site's own style. A high-specificity rule in `<style id="nov14-v2">` keeps the page upright even where the external `r15` stylesheets force italics; the Julie fold's `.serif-i` spans are the only intentional italics.
- Pricing accent colours: blue `#0a5bd8` / `#2F7BFF`, violet `#7C5CFF` (VIP), green for the discount chip.
